What the ads will look like

Previews use venue-style creative aligned to your site messaging (“Where Beauty Meets Celebration,” capacity, garden / water / mountain views). Final ads will use your real photos and short video (same authentic on-location look your management guide recommends). These are mock Meta placements — not live campaigns.

What I will do

Setup (week 1)

  • Confirm Meta Business access (Page, Instagram, Ads Manager, Pixel)
  • Check Meta Pixel on Squarespace and that the inquiry / “Schedule a Quick Call” path is trackable
  • Select 3–6 of your strongest venue photos (and a short clip if available)
  • Build the first Ads Manager campaign (not a simple “boost post”)

Creative

  • 2–3 ad variations at launch (different photo or photo + short video)
  • Copy drawn from your site: garden estate · Tacoma · water & mountain views · up to 180 guests · limited events per year
  • Clear CTA: Book a Tour / Schedule a Quick Call → damiansvenue.com

Audience & placements

  • Greater Puget Sound (Tacoma, Seattle, and surrounding)
  • People planning weddings / events (Meta interests + Advantage+ where useful)
  • Feed, Stories, and Reels placements for venue photography

Ongoing

  • Watch Results and Cost per Result after a few days — not day-one vanity metrics
  • Pause weak creatives; keep or refresh winners every few weeks
  • Once Pixel has data: retarget people who visited the site but didn’t inquire
  • Short written update: what’s running, spend, inquiries, next change

Organic posting and day-to-day Squarespace edits are out of scope for this ads-only proposal unless we add them later.

What you pay for

This is a standalone Meta Ads retainer — not a website package, hosting plan, or organic social plan.

  • Meta Ads retainer — campaign setup, creative, optimization, and reporting
  • Meta ad spend — paid by you directly to Meta (separate from the retainer; I don’t mark up media)

What I need from you

  • Admin access to Meta Business Suite / Ads Manager
  • High-resolution photos (and video if you have it)
  • Preferred destination: contact form or Schedule a Quick Call link
  • Agreed ad spend budget with Meta for the period we run ads

Next step

If this looks right, we’ll schedule a short kickoff, get Business Manager access, pick the first photo set, and go live with the feed + Stories creatives above (using your real assets). Retainer terms are confirmed on the kickoff call.
