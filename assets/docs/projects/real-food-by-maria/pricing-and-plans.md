### Essential Care

- Website and app stay hosted, secure and online
- App Store and Play Store updates 4 times a year
- Questions answered in 5 business days
- If your app is down, a reply in 1 business day (weekdays only)
- Content changes and new features are quoted separately

### Standard Care — everything in Essential, plus

- Content updates included: send photos and videos any time, published every month
- App store updates every month instead of 4 times a year
- New features and improvements on request, plus one major update each year (like a page redesign, or new pages or tools), with no separate bill
- If your app is down, a reply in 4 hours instead of 1 business day, nights and weekends included
- Questions answered in 2 business days instead of 5
- Your requests move ahead of Essential clients

### Priority Care — everything in Standard, plus

- Content updates every week instead of every month
- Two major updates a year instead of one
- If your app is down, a reply in 2 hours instead of 4, nights and weekends included
- Questions answered in 3 business hours instead of 2 business days
- First in line, ahead of every other client

> You add your own lessons, videos and live classes from your dashboard on every plan. "Content updates" means changes to the pages themselves (photos, text, sections) that we do for you.

## Step 5 — Ownership: what's yours and what's ours

| Yours | Ours (Code With Ruben) |
| --- | --- |
| Your domain name | The platform: the code, design and building blocks behind the website and apps |
| Your content: videos, recipes, photos, written text and downloads | Hosting setup |
| Your brand: name, logo and photos of you | The App Store and Google Play listings and accounts |
| Your Stripe account and every payment members make | |

**In plain words**

- **You own everything you make and teach.** Your videos, recipes and brand are yours, and you can use them anywhere, on this platform or any other.
- **We own the software that shows it.** When the build is paid in full, you get a license to use the platform for your business, for as long as you keep a care plan. This is how we keep the price at $2,000 instead of a full custom build, and why updates, hosting and support are handled for you.
- **If you stop the care plan,** the license ends and the website and apps go offline. Your domain, content, brand and Stripe account stay yours.
- **If you'd rather own the software too,** there's an Ownership Upgrade (buyout): we hand over the code, files, store listings and accounts, and from then on you or your own developer run it. It's quoted on request.

## Paso 5 — Propiedad: qué es tuyo y qué es nuestro

| Tuyo | Nuestro (Code With Ruben) |
| --- | --- |
| Tu dominio | La plataforma: el código, el diseño y las piezas detrás del sitio y las apps |
| Tu contenido: videos, recetas, fotos, textos y descargas | La configuración del hospedaje |
| Tu marca: nombre, logo y fotos tuyas | Las cuentas y fichas en App Store y Google Play |
| Tu cuenta de Stripe y cada pago de tus miembros | |

**En pocas palabras**

- **Todo lo que creas y enseñas es tuyo.** Tus videos, recetas y marca son tuyos y los puedes usar donde quieras, en esta plataforma o en cualquier otra.
- **El programa que lo muestra es nuestro.** Cuando la construcción está pagada completa, recibes una licencia para usar la plataforma en tu negocio mientras mantengas un plan de mantenimiento. Así mantenemos el precio en $2,000 en lugar de una construcción a la medida completa, y por eso las actualizaciones, el hospedaje y el soporte los manejamos nosotros.
- **Si dejas el plan de mantenimiento,** la licencia termina y el sitio y las apps se desconectan. Tu dominio, contenido, marca y cuenta de Stripe siguen siendo tuyos.
- **Si prefieres ser dueña del programa también,** existe la Compra de propiedad: te entregamos el código, archivos, fichas y cuentas de las tiendas, y desde ahí tú o tu propio programador lo manejan. Se cotiza cuando lo pidas.
