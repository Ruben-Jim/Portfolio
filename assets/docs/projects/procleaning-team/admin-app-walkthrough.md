## For owners

- Android staff app is **Play Closed testing**, not a public store listing. Invite each phone’s Play Gmail, then: [Become a tester](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.procleaning.app) → [Install](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.procleaning.app).
- **Update ready** → Restart. A new Play build is only for icon, splash, or sign-in baked into the app.
- Changes in Dash and Orders show on the site and for workers.
- Deleting a staff account removes login only — not job history.
